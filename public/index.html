Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our authentic Brahmin recipes, shelf life, and order shipping.

What is the shelf life of Sri Skanda Podis and Sweets?

Our spiced powders (Podis) remain fresh for up to 6 months when stored in airtight containers away from moisture. Traditional sweets prepared with pure cow ghee and organic jaggery stay fresh for 30 to 45 days.

Are your products 100% Pure Brahmin & Garlic-Free?

Yes, absolutely! All our traditional podis and sweets are prepared following strict South Indian Brahmin culinary heritage with zero garlic, artificial flavorings, colors, or preservatives.

How are orders packed and shipped across India?

Every order is freshly prepared upon confirmation and packed in food-grade, moisture-proof sealed pouches. We dispatch nationwide via express courier services, delivering within 2 to 4 business days.

Can I request custom sweetener levels or bulk event boxes?

Yes! We prepare custom millet sweets (Ragi, Foxtail, Kodo laddus) with organic jaggery, dates & nuts (sugar-free), or country sugar. We also provide customized festival & wedding gift boxes. Contact us on WhatsApp for bulk quotes.
